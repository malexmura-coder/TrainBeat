# TrainBeat privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

TrainBeat is a practice looper for Android, written by Alessandro Mura. It is free
software (GPL-3.0-or-later); the source code is at
https://github.com/malexmura-coder/TrainBeat.

## In short

TrainBeat does not collect, store on a server, transmit or share any personal data.
It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
It does not use the network: it has no internet permission at all.

## What stays on your device

- **Your music files.** The app reads the audio files, scores and folders you choose,
  through Android's own file picker. It only reads what you grant, and only to play,
  analyse or cut it. Files you cut or record are written to the folder you choose.
- **Settings and the library list.** The tempo and key of your tracks, your favourites,
  and the folders you granted are kept in the app's private storage on the device.
  Uninstalling the app removes them.
- **Recordings.** The microphone is used only when you press Record in the loop
  recorder. The recording is saved as a file in the folder you choose and is never sent
  anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Microphone**: only for the loop recorder, as above.
- **Notifications and foreground service**: to keep playback running with the screen
  off, with a notification that stops it, and to show the progress of a library scan.
- **Bluetooth media keys and pedals** work through Android's standard media session; the
  app asks for no Bluetooth permission.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
