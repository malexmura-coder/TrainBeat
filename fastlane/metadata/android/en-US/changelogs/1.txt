First release.

Gapless looping of WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG and MIDI. Speed from 50% to 150%,
transposition from -7 to +4 semitones, and a train mode that raises the tempo one
repetition at a time. Optional offline rendering for a cleaner slow-down.

MIDI files are played with a SoundFont of your choosing, with tempo and key read
from the file. A library scan remembers tempo and key per file. A trimmer cuts
seamless loops out of longer tracks.
