Playback no longer stops when the screen goes off. A loop keeps running in the
background behind a notification that can stop it, and Bluetooth media keys and
page-turner pedals drive it.

A cursor shows where the loop is and sets where Play comes in, so stopping is
pausing and Play carries on from the same bar.

Audio files and scores sent in from other apps open straight in the player.

On a MIDI file, single instruments can be switched off.
