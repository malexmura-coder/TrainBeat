A practice player for musicians: it opens an audio file or a MIDI score, loops it
without a gap, and lets you slow it down, transpose it, or let the tempo climb one
repetition at a time.

The loop has no seam. A single audio track is fed by a wrap-around buffer, so the
sample after the last one is the first one again and the stream never stops. That
matters when what you are looping is a single bar you intend to play over for
twenty minutes.

Speed and key are applied by the device while playing, which is instant. For short
loops a "hi-fi" switch prepares the material offline instead, with a phase vocoder
that resets its phases on every drum hit, so a slowed-down groove keeps its
attacks.

Train mode raises the tempo by a fixed step every repetition, so you can start
under tempo and let the piece carry you up to it.

MIDI files are treated as what they are: scores. Changing the tempo is a
multiplication and transposing is an addition, so both are exact - no stretching,
no artefacts - and tempo, metre and key are read from the file rather than guessed
at. To hear a score you choose a SoundFont (.sf2) once; the app ships none,
because a General MIDI bank is 30 to 150 MB and belongs to whoever made it.

The library scans the folders you grant and remembers the tempo and key of every
file, so nothing is analysed while you are trying to play. Any key can be
corrected by hand.

The loop trimmer cuts a loop out of a longer track. On audio it snaps to zero
crossings and looks for the end that matches the start; on a MIDI file it cuts on
bar lines, which is exact by construction, and can save either a WAV or a MIDI
that stays adjustable for ever.

No network access, no advertising, no analytics, no accounts. The app never
connects to anything.
